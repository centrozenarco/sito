Eijun Linda Cutts

Dharma Master Eijun Linda Cutts began practicing Zen with Shunryu Suzuki Roshi in 1971. She was ordained as a Zen priest in 1975, and received the Dharma lineage from Reb Anderson Tenshin Roshi, a disciple of Suzuki Roshi, in 1996. From 2000 to 2007 she served as co-abbess of the San Francisco Zen Center. From 2010 to 2014 she served as abiding Abbes of Green Gulch, and from 2014 to 2019 she has been Central Abbess of the San Francisco Zen Center.
